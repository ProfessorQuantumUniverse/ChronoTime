## 🔮 About The App

**ChronoTime** is not just a clock; it is a kinetic sculpture of time for your Android device. 

Built with a stunning **Liquid Glass** design language, the app utilizes **AGSL Shaders** to create living, breathing backgrounds that react to your touch. Every interaction is paired with rich, immersive **haptic feedback**, making time feel tangible.

Forget standard ticking. Swipe vertically to travel through different dimensions of time measurement, from historical artifacts like the *Berlin Clock* to developer-centric *Unix Epochs*.

---

## 🚀 Features

*   **✨ Liquid Glass UI:** A modern, translucent interface that feels premium and futuristic.
*   **🎨 AGSL Powered:** Real-time shaders run in the background, providing smooth, battery-efficient animations.
*   **📳 Haptic Experience:** Every tick, swipe, and interaction provides satisfying tactile feedback.
*   **👆 Vertical Navigation:** Seamlessly swipe up/down to cycle through 8 distinct clock engines.
*   **🕶️ Focus Mode:** Tap to hide the UI and let the time visualization take over the full screen.
*   **🟢 Matrix Mode:** Enter the code. A stylized digital rain aesthetic.
*   **⚙️ Custom Settings:** Tweak the experience to fit your needs.

---

## 🕰️ The 8 Clock Faces

ChronoTime includes 8 unique ways to visualize the passing of time:

| Icon | Name | Description |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 🕐 | **Modern Standard** | A clean, stylish take on the traditional clock with various style options. |
| 🎨 | **Hex Clock** | For designers. Displays the time as a changing Hex Color Code. |
| 🔟 | **Binary Clock** | Read time in the language of computers (0s and 1s). |
| 🚦 | **Berlin Clock** | A faithful recreation of the famous *Mengenlehreuhr* in Berlin. |
| ☀️ | **Solar Stand** | Visualizes time based on the sun's position and daylight cycle. |
| ⏳ | **Sand & Water** | A fluid simulation showing how much of the day has flowed away. |
| 🤖 | **Unix Epoch** | The raw flow of seconds since January 1, 1970. |
| 💾 | **Matrix** | Digital aesthetic mode for the sci-fi enthusiast. |
